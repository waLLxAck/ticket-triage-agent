# Why was my card declined?
Category: card_payment

Common reasons: insufficient balance, the card is frozen in the app, the merchant is in a blocked
country, or the payment exceeds your daily card limit (£2,000 on Standard, £5,000 on Premium).
Check Cards → Card status in the app. If the card is frozen, tap Unfreeze. If the limit is the
problem, you can raise it temporarily under Cards → Limits for 24 hours.
