# Fees and limits
Category: fees_limits

Standard plan: no monthly fee, free UK transfers, 1 free ATM withdrawal of up to £200 a month, then 2% per
withdrawal. Foreign card payments carry a 0.5% fee. Premium plan (£7/month): unlimited free
ATM withdrawals up to £800 a month and no foreign payment fee. Daily card limit is £2,000 on
Standard and £5,000 on Premium.
